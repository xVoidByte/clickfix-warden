You are protected

ClickFix Warden now watches for pages that try to trick you into running commands, and warns you about sites reported as malicious.

Everything is checked inside your browser, nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere.

ClickFix Warden needs access to all websites to protect them.

Some settings are managed by your organisation.

      About

      ClickFix Warden collects no data. It has no account, no telemetry and no server of its own.

      The only thing it downloads is the signed database and rules from GitHub, which sees the same request any update makes.
