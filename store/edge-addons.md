# Microsoft Edge Add-ons

Upload `.output/clickfix-warden-<version>-edge.zip` in Partner Center at https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/microsoftedge/overview. Registration is free.

## Properties

- Category, pick the closest one to security that the list offers at the time of upload
- Privacy policy, required, https://xvoidbyte.github.io/ClickFixWarden/privacy.html
- Website, https://github.com/xVoidByte/ClickFixWarden
- Support contact, https://github.com/xVoidByte/ClickFixWarden/issues
- Mature content, no

## Store listings

Add English.

- Description, from `listing.md`
- Short description, the 132 character line from `listing.md`
- Logo, `store/screenshots/logo-300.png`
- Screenshots, `store/screenshots/1-*.png` to `5-*.png`
- Small promotional tile, `store/screenshots/promo-440x280.png`
