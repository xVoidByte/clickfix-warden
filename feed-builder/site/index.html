ClickFix Warden

A free, open source browser extension that stops ClickFix and fake CAPTCHA attacks, the pages that trick you into pasting a command into Run, Terminal or File Explorer, and warns you about sites reported as malicious in public threat databases.

Privacy first. No account, no telemetry, no server of its own. Every check runs inside your browser. Privacy policy

Database. This site hosts the signed database and detection rules the extension downloads every hour, built from the public URLhaus, ThreatFox and Feodo Tracker projects by abuse.ch.
